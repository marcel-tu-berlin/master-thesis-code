And success sits strictly below termination on three of the four eval families, so removing non-termination is not the same act as increasing task success; on a set where every terminated episode is correct this condition would be indistinguishable from a task reward and RQ2 would have nothing to measure. The penalty adds a third level to what was a two-level ordering: non-terminating below terminated-but-wrong below correct. RQ2's prediction to test is the reverse of E2's - if premature stopping falls, does completion length rise, the agent buying termination with tokens? e27's held_out baseline is 1711 mean tokens at 1.78 steps.
