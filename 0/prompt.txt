2. Key Technical Concepts:
   - **TRL 1.6 vLLM TIS (truncated importance sampling) correction - the found root cause:** vLLM samples rollouts; the trainer recomputes those tokens' logps with its HF forward at the same weights; per-token abs disagreement ~0.018 (numerics). Default `vllm_importance_sampling_mode="sequence_mask"` computes a per-EPISODE weight `exp(SIGNED per-token drift summed over all assistant tokens)`, and `masked_fill(0.0)` outside [min, 3.0]. Applied at `grpo_trainer.py:2613`: `per_token_loss = per_token_loss * inputs["importance_sampling_ratio"]`. The summed drift grows with completion length -> long episodes lose gradient preferentially. `token_truncate` instead clamps per-token ratios (shape [B, T], nearly all ~1.0); `"off"` maps to `vllm_importance_sampling_correction=False`.
   - **Measured ISR run means:** e27bs4 0.283, e28bs4 0.291, e29bs4 0.282 (browsergym); e24bs4 poly 0.486. Min 0.0000 nearly every step, max pinned at 2.996. Bad from step 1 (not staleness).
