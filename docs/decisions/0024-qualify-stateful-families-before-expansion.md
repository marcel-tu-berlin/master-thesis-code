# Qualify stateful families before expanding the experiment

Decided: 2026-09-29, following the user's request to extend the family selection.

Retain the completed read-table-2 E0/E1/E2 comparison at seed 4016 as the first,
narrow piece of thesis evidence. Fast interface learning limits its scope; it does
not by itself invalidate the result. Preserve its frozen artifacts and findings.

Qualify additional MiniWoB families with scripted correct and wrong paths, then
bounded base-policy samples through the native training path. Judge candidates by
state-dependent decisions, task-success variation and successful-response length
variation. Extra clicks and shuffled layouts alone do not establish the desired
behavioral complexity. Interface and budget constraints that can be repaired are
engineering work, not grounds for rejecting an otherwise useful task.

The extension may use visible HTML where accessibility text omits custom action
IDs. Hidden state must remain hidden. Freeze observation format, tools, prompt,
budgets and task mapping across the next comparison. This is a new protocol;
historical reproduction uses the original source. Keep the pinned tasks and native
scorers unchanged, and record any scoring limitation that affects interpretation.

The user authorized qualification and preparation, including GPU probes, but
explicitly withheld approval for the next experiment batch. One-step base-policy
screens use the existing zero-LR warmup capture and must prove unchanged trainable
parameter hashes. They are diagnostic samples, not E1 training results. Small
screens cannot demonstrate that a family will remain unsaturated after training.

The user explicitly restricted qualification to GPU 1 after the longer-budget
memory failures. Keep that allocation. Saved-tensor replays may test memory
without new generation, but cropped replay tensors are not task-performance data.

Prepare a concrete proposal with qualified tasks, matched E0/E1/E2 configs,
measurement limitations and verification evidence. Then stop for the user's final
approval. Do not launch the proposed E0, E1 or E2, other doses, replications or E3
automatically. Decisions 0021/0022/0023 continue to govern the retained first trio
and its deferred extensions.
